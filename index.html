Key workflow

From official source to HR action

1

Monitor

Fetch official legislative and agency pages for covered jurisdictions. Store immutable snapshots and feed-health status.

2

Extract

Classify labor-law changes and extract dates, affected employers, penalties, and action requirements using cited source text.

3

Review

Human reviewers approve or edit every summary until accuracy clears 95% across 100 consecutive alerts.

4

Act

Slack/email alerts create owned checklist tasks and a legal-ready audit trail.
