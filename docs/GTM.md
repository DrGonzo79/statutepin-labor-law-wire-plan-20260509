# Go-To-Market — Statutepin

## Positioning

“Reviewed labor-law alerts for remote teams — before the next payroll cycle.”

Avoid: “AI legal research.”
Use: “Human-reviewed compliance operations alerts with checklists and audit proof.”

## ICP segments

1. Remote-first SaaS companies, 50–300 employees, employees in 5+ states.
2. Professional services firms hiring nationally but without a deep HR compliance team.
3. Fractional HR consultants managing multiple clients.
4. Payroll consultants who see recurring state-compliance fires.

## Offer ladder

- **Lead magnet:** Free Compliance Quick-Scan PDF / form: “Which states do you hire in? We’ll show the next 5 likely compliance checks.”
- **Basic:** $299/month, up to 5 states, email/Slack alerts, dashboard, audit log.
- **Pro:** $499/month, up to 10 states, BambooHR/CSV employee-location sync, advanced routing.
- **Services add-on:** $1,500 setup for custom jurisdictions, policy inventory, and first-month compliance mapping.

## Channels

- HR communities: SHRM local groups, People Ops Slack communities, Reddit HR threads, LinkedIn HR operators.
- Content: “What changed this month in CA/NY/WA labor rules and who must act.”
- Partner channel: payroll consultants, HR compliance advisors, fractional People Ops firms.
- Outbound: remote-first companies with public multi-state job postings.
- SEO later: target “employment law compliance,” “employee labor laws,” “HR alerts,” “labor compliance service.”

## Sales motion

1. Free five-state quick scan.
2. 30-day monitored pilot with Slack/email alerts.
3. Review one real alert in a 20-minute call.
4. Convert to paid when one action is created.
5. Upsell Pro when employee locations need automated sync.

## Proof assets

- Sample alert with citations and action checklist.
- Audit-log screenshot.
- Monthly state-change digest.
- “Penalty avoided” ROI calculator.
- Pilot scorecard: alerts sent, actions completed, legal escalations avoided.
